# AI Engineering — Evidence Ladder

Updated: 2026-10-27

## Why this is a separate track

RAMACH's long-term ambition can include architecture, structural, MEP and documentation automation. The risk is making the claim before the evidence exists. The correct strategy is to build an evidence ladder.

## What is demonstrably shipping in 2026

### Early architecture / plan generation
- Maket: editable residential layouts; explicitly not construction documents and no local code/zoning verification.
- Autodesk Forma Building Layout Explorer: experimental multifamily layout exploration; Autodesk calls it a starting point, not a reliable final output.
- Finch: structured building generation, data, BIM geometry, firm systems and compliance-oriented workflows.
- Snaptrude: AI inside an editable connected architectural workspace.
- Planner 5D: AI visual/design workspace including floor-plan tooling and rendering.

### Project/context AI
- Autodesk and Procore are both moving toward agents grounded in connected project data/context.
- Buildots/OpenSpace/Doxel use site imagery + BIM/schedule context to turn reality capture into project intelligence.

## What RAMACH should not claim yet

Without reproducible evidence, do not claim:
- autonomous full building engineering
- code-compliant permit-ready design
- structural safety
- coordinated MEP ready for construction
- automatic sign-off or replacement of licensed professionals

## Evidence ladder for RAMACH

### Level 0 — Research
Demo/model only. No commercial promise.

### Level 1 — Assistant
AI proposes options or checks; professional decides.
Evidence: benchmark set + documented failure cases.

### Level 2 — Structured production
AI produces editable CAD/BIM objects or calculations, not images only.
Evidence: deterministic geometry/data validation and export.

### Level 3 — Rule-grounded
System checks explicit local constraints/codes.
Evidence: traceable rule references, test suite, false-positive/negative tracking.

### Level 4 — Multi-discipline coordination
Architecture + structure + MEP constraints operate on shared model.
Evidence: clash/constraint tests, professional peer review, multiple project types.

### Level 5 — Engineering-grade automation
Outputs consistently approach professional construction-document quality within a defined project envelope.
Evidence: third-party review, real projects, liability/process definition, change tracking, audit trail.

## Recommended internal principle

**Strong before first. Evidence before marketing.**

Do not compete on the sentence “AI designs buildings.” Compete on verifiable output quality, editability, traceability and integration with real project context.

## Strategic opportunity

If RAMACH develops a shared project model first, future AI does not start from a blank prompt. It can reason over:
- site/terrain/context
- project brief
- zoning/rules
- structured geometry
- revisions/decisions
- materials
- cost/schedule
- field evidence
- team roles

That is far more defensible than a standalone image or plan generator.
