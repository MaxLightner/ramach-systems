# Iran Competitive Landscape — First Pass

Updated: 2026-10-27

This is a first-pass landscape, not a final market map. Numbers quoted below are vendor claims unless independently verified.

## 1. Visualization / AR / VR

### Zi1Plus / زیوان پلاس
Positioning: architectural VR/AR before construction. Promises realistic spatial presence, walking inside the building, object interaction and environmental/light experience.

Strategic signal for RAMACH:
- VR-before-build is already a known Iranian proposition.
- RAMACH cannot differentiate by saying “we show architecture in VR.”
- Differentiation must come from integration with the live project, higher-end visualization, site context, or workflow continuity.

Source: https://ir.linkedin.com/company/zi1plus

### VRHouse
Positioning: 3D/360 virtual tours for property and agencies, with measurement, plan generation, tags, video output, downloadable 3D model and agency management.

Strategic signal:
- virtual-tour real estate is a mature service category locally.
- strong evidence that a simple “virtual visit” is not a moat.
- useful benchmark for property marketing, not the same product as Archiyar.

Source: https://vrhouse.ir/

### PropTour
Positioning: 3D property visits, pre-sales visualization and future interactive redesign.

Strategic signal:
- pre-sale and visualization language is already consumer-readable.
- RAMACH should distinguish “future building in its real site context” from generic 360 tours.

Source: https://proptour.ir/

### Omran Naghsh Sadra
Positioning: BIM services with 4D schedule, 5D cost, documentation, AR overlay and VR tours.

Strategic signal:
- AR/VR can be bundled as BIM services.
- “BIM + AR + VR” by itself is not a differentiated category.

Source: https://onsadra.ir/

## 2. AI / computational architecture

### Ziggurat / زیگورات
Positioning claim: design + construction using AI, VR, IT and data engineering.

Strategic signal:
- Iranian companies already market “AI architecture.”
- RAMACH should not enter a claims race; proof and productization matter more than wording.

Source: https://ziggurat-co.com/

### Novaco project for Saba Naft
A 2026 project publicly describes an agentic AI assistant for building massing, floor-plan layout and functional analysis, including Iranian urban rules and National Building Regulations Part 4. The published result describes an MVP with massing and automated compliance evaluation.

Strategic signal:
- localized Iranian regulation reasoning is already being actively pursued.
- AI design in Iran is not hypothetical research only.
- RAMACH should benchmark actual output quality and scope before defining an AI-engineering roadmap.

Source: https://novaco-ahdaf.com/en/projects/design-and-implementation-of-an-agentic-ai-assistant-for-automated-building-massing-floor-plan-layout-generation-and-fun

### Architalog
Positioning: digital architecture showroom, real Iranian brand objects, standardized files and AI moodboards connecting architects/designers with showrooms.

Strategic signal:
- valuable local data can be a stronger moat than generic AI.
- manufacturer objects/material catalogs could later connect to Kitchyar/Chido/design AI.

Source: https://architalog.com/

### iArch Lab — Iran University of Science and Technology
Research lab founded in 2024 around computational design, AI, data analysis, parametric tools and smart buildings.

Strategic signal:
- potential research/validation ecosystem, not merely a commercial competitor.
- useful source for academic benchmarking and recruitment/collaboration.

Source: https://iarchlab.com/

## 3. Project management / BIM

### Soha / سها
Positioning: integrated construction project management across time, cost, resources and progress; web-based and customizable.

Strategic signal:
- project dashboards and management are established categories.
- Kamcheh should not become a generic PM clone.

Source: https://iksoha.com/our-services/customization/construction/

### PTEC BIM services
Positioning: BIM modeling, coordination/clash detection, quantity/cost estimation and as-built information.

Strategic signal:
- standard BIM service stack is commoditized.
- RAMACH should focus on interaction, field use, project identity, and cross-product continuity.

Source: https://ptec.ir/%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-bim/

## 4. Networks / marketplaces / supply

### Civil808
Long-running Persian-language social network for civil and architectural engineers.

Strategic signal:
- “social network for engineers” is not new.
- a new RAMACH social layer needs transactional/project utility, not a generic feed.

Source: https://ir.linkedin.com/company/civil808

### ETOOOD
Architecture communication platform with professional projects, profiles, articles and forums; founded as a bridge among contemporary Iranian architects.

Strategic signal:
- architecture publishing/community is established.
- RAMACH should not recreate a portfolio feed unless it is attached to verified project data, work opportunities or tools.

Source: https://etoood.com/

### Archiz
Architect discovery and remote project marketplace with profile/sample-work listings and categories including plan design, villa, BIM, phase two, detailing and parametric design.

Strategic signal:
- architect-client matching exists.
- marketplace differentiation requires verified competence, project history, workflow integration or better matching data.

Source: https://archizco.com/SearchArchitect/Index

### Tosee Sakhteman / توسعه ساختمان
B2B platform connecting projects, factories, engineers and contractors, with active project procurement and supplier discovery.

Strategic signal:
- supplier/contractor networking is already being pursued at national scale.
- Kamcheh needs a sharper wedge than “connect industry actors.”

Source: https://www.toseesakhteman.com/

### Kala-ba-Kala / کالا با کالا
Marketplace connecting contractors, employers, suppliers, machinery and projects. Site claims 500+ active contractors and 1,200+ listings.

Strategic signal:
- contractor/supplier/machinery marketplace is competitive.
- evidence and transaction trust may be more important than listing volume.

Source: https://www.kala-bakala.com/about

### Moshtareim / مشتریم
Specialized price inquiry and tender platform for Iran's construction industry.

Strategic signal:
- procurement/tender is a standalone wedge.
- potentially complementary to project identity rather than something RAMACH must rebuild.

Source: https://moshtareim.ir/

### Achareh
Horizontal local-services marketplace including repair, construction, renovation and interior services; has operated specialized building/renovation work as well.

Strategic signal:
- small-job household services are not the correct battlefield for Kamcheh if RAMACH targets project execution.
- RAMACH should preserve its distinction between construction execution and handyman services.

Sources:
https://achareh.co/
https://ir.linkedin.com/company/acharehofficial

## Initial Iran conclusion

Iran already has separate players for:
- VR/AR visualization
- BIM services
- project management
- AI design claims
- architecture publishing/community
- architect freelancing
- contractor/supplier marketplaces
- tender/procurement

The apparent gap is **not absence of features**. The hypothesis worth testing is absence of a coherent project-centered system connecting design geometry, site context, field execution, people, evidence, procurement, presentation and future AI.
