# Global Competitive Landscape — First Pass

Updated: 2026-10-27

## 1. Connected AEC platforms

### Autodesk Forma / Construction
Autodesk is explicitly extending a connected AEC cloud across early planning, detailed design and construction. 2026 announcements add more AI and connected workflows, with Revit and Civil 3D tied into Forma and project context carried forward.

Strategic signal:
- “one connected building lifecycle” is a major global direction, not a unique thesis.
- RAMACH cannot out-platform Autodesk globally; it can win via Iran localization, narrower workflows, UX, site-native spatial tools, or a different distribution model.
- interoperability should be a design principle from day one.

Sources:
https://adsknews.autodesk.com/en/news/autodesk-forma-ai-aec-connected-workflows-2026/
https://construction.autodesk.com/

### Procore
Procore is evolving its common data environment toward connected data and agentic AI coworkers acting across construction workflows, while keeping professional control.

Strategic signal:
- project data/context is becoming the substrate for agents.
- persistent project identity is strategically aligned with where large platforms are going.

Source: https://www.procore.com/press/procore-redefines-the-common-data-environment-with-connected-data-and-agentic-ai

### PlanRadar
Field/project management around tasks, plan pins, deadlines, chat, reports, documentation and BIM.

Strategic signal:
- task/report/document management is a mature category.
- RAMACH should avoid rebuilding generic issue tracking unless spatial/project context makes it materially better.

Source: https://www.planradar.com/us/product/construction-management-software/

## 2. Reality capture / construction intelligence

### OpenSpace
360 capture mapped automatically to plans/BIM, with BIM Compare and site navigation; also mobile/LiDAR scanning.

### Buildots
AI construction intelligence: site imagery from 360/drone sources compared to BIM/schedule, automated progress tracking and delay forecasting.

### Doxel
Computer vision progress tracking from 360 video, installed quantity measurement, pay/change-order validation, live as-built visualization.

### Cupix
4D spatial intelligence from 360 video, repeated reality capture, measurements, BIM, issue tracking and time-based site records.

Strategic signal across all four:
- camera/site capture is increasingly a data collection layer, not only visualization.
- future Archiyar/camera-tracking work should distinguish:
  1. visualization of proposed design in place,
  2. documentation of existing reality,
  3. automated comparison/progress intelligence.
- the third category requires substantial CV/BIM infrastructure and should not be implied without evidence.

https://buildots.com/product/
https://doxel.ai/faqs
https://www.cupix.com/

## 3. AI design / authoring

### Finch
AI-native building authoring platform with floor plans, area calculations, BIM geometry, reusable firm design systems, code/compliance workflows and Revit/Rhino/Grasshopper interoperability.

### Snaptrude
Cloud-native AEC design workspace with AI that works on connected editable program/design/detail/documentation context.

### Autodesk Forma Building Layout Explorer
Experimental generative AI for multifamily floor-plan exploration from massing and program/unit-mix requirements; Autodesk itself describes current output as a starting point, not something to rely on directly.

### Maket
Residential plan generation/editing + renders. Maket explicitly says its outputs are design-stage starting points, not construction documents; it does not verify local codes/zoning and still requires professional review.

### Planner 5D AI Studio
AI workspace for rendering, image/material editing, floor-plan generation/enhancement, video and visual iteration.

Strategic signal:
- AI plan generation is already becoming commodity functionality.
- stronger frontier = editable structured geometry + rules + BIM + project knowledge + downstream engineering.
- credible vendors explicitly state boundaries; RAMACH should do the same.

## 4. Professional/community/network models

### Houzz
Connects homeowners with building/design professionals and vendors; also operates Houzz Pro software. Houzz says its community includes millions of professionals and tens of millions of homeowners/design enthusiasts.

### Architizer
Architecture project/firm publishing ecosystem with a large visual library, professional community, awards and product discovery.

### BuildZoom
Uses licensing/building-department/consumer data to help owners select contractors, with project consultants; monetizes successful contractor hires via commission.

Strategic signal:
- successful networks are tied to a job:
  - inspiration + hiring (Houzz)
  - professional visibility + product discovery (Architizer)
  - contractor selection + transaction (BuildZoom)
- “social network for construction” is too vague. RAMACH must choose a high-value graph edge and workflow.

Sources:
https://www.houzz.com/aboutUs
https://architizer.com/about/
https://www.buildzoom.com/help_center_topics/8

## Global conclusion

The direction of travel is converging:
**connected project data + structured geometry + reality capture + AI agents + collaboration.**

RAMACH should not compete by copying the breadth of global incumbents. It should identify a localized wedge where its existing spatial/product capabilities give it an unfair starting advantage, then ensure all products write into a shared project context.
