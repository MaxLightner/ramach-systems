# RAMACH Strategy Research Charter

Updated: 2026-10-27

## Core question

RAMACH should not be defined by a list of technologies. The research asks:

> Across the building lifecycle — land / brief → design → engineering → presentation → procurement → construction → project memory → sales / operation — where are the largest unresolved workflows, who pays to solve them, and which connected system could RAMACH credibly own in Iran first and later expand globally?

## Decisions this research must support

1. What kind of company is RAMACH?
2. Who is the initial buyer and user?
3. Which problem is the wedge / entry point?
4. What should be products vs shared infrastructure?
5. What should the persistent project identity/data model contain?
6. Where can AI be useful today, and what evidence threshold is required before RAMACH claims engineering capability?
7. What network effects are genuinely valuable in construction?
8. Which capabilities are premium visualization services vs scalable software?
9. How do Archiyar, Chido, Kitchyar, Kamcheh, Mizato, web/timeline and future AI connect?
10. What moat is hard to copy in Iran and still relevant globally?

## Research lenses

### A. Iran
- AI architecture/design claims
- BIM and project management
- AR / VR / virtual tours / reality capture
- professional networks and architecture publishing
- contractor / supplier / workforce marketplaces
- local procurement and tender platforms
- local building-code and approval workflows

### B. Global
- Autodesk Forma / ACC
- Procore / PlanRadar
- Buildots / OpenSpace / Doxel / Cupix
- Finch / Snaptrude / Maket / Planner 5D
- Houzz / Architizer / BuildZoom
- reality capture, spatial computing and digital twins

### C. Emerging
- agentic AEC
- automated floor plans and BIM generation
- code / zoning reasoning
- computer vision progress tracking
- Gaussian Splatting / NeRF for site context
- camera tracking / AR site visualization
- AI-assisted structural and MEP engineering
- project knowledge graph / persistent building identity

## Evaluation model

Each competitor/opportunity is scored qualitatively on:
- buyer pain severity
- frequency of use
- willingness to pay
- local Iran fit
- defensibility
- data/network advantage
- integration potential with RAMACH
- maturity: shipping / experimental / claimed
- evidence quality

## Important constraint

RAMACH should aim to be one of the strongest systems, not the first to make an unproven claim. AI engineering claims require evidence: benchmark projects, constraint compliance, professional review, repeatability and explicit failure boundaries.

## Working hypothesis

The strongest long-term opportunity may not be a single app. It may be a **persistent digital identity for each project** that connects geometry, site context, people, decisions, timeline, cost, procurement, field evidence and AI. This hypothesis is not yet accepted; research must try to falsify it.
